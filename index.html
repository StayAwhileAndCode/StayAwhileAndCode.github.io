A fruit fly brain is playing Diablo

LIVE ON TWITCH

Scientists mapped every connection in a real fruit fly's brain. A simulated copy of that wiring now plays a Diablo hero on its own, all day long.

The panel shows what it senses: a monster closing in, getting hit, loot nearby. And what it decides: fight, grab, flee. I don't control it. It dies a lot. It keeps going deeper.
